# Fabian MVP

## The one loop

A parent gets a drill, decides scam or real, sees the tell, and keeps a streak. The payer sees her progress. If this loop does not hold people for 14 days, nothing else matters.

## In scope (4 screens)

1. Payer setup: create a family, send the parent a magic link. No passwords.
2. Parent's daily drill: one message, big Scam and Real buttons, then the tell and what to do.
3. Payer dashboard: streak, score, weakest scam type.
4. A fixed 20-message check on day 1 and day 14, so there is a before and after.

## Content

About 60 drills, mixed scam and real. Categories: bank alerts, package and toll texts, grandparent emergencies, tech support, Medicare and Social Security, romance, crypto and investment, voice-clone calls. Each drill has: channel, text, answer, the tell, what to do.

## The 14-day test

20 to 30 parents from DC's network. Measure:

- Share of parents who finish 5 of the first 7 days
- Day 1 vs day 14 score on the same 20 messages
- Share of payers who say they would pay, and how much

Pass bar to be set by DC before the test starts.
